# Product Thinking Resources

## Knowledge

- [Article: "The Four Big Risks" by Marty Cagan (SVPG, 2017)](https://www.svpg.com/four-big-risks/)
  Short, foundational framing: value, usability, feasibility, and viability risk. Use for: deciding what to test before building.
- [Book: _The Mom Test_ by Rob Fitzpatrick](https://www.momtestbook.com/)
  How to talk to potential users without getting polite, misleading answers. Use for: testing value risk.
- [Article: "Know Your Customers' Jobs to Be Done" by Clayton Christensen et al. (HBR, 2016)](https://hbr.org/2016/09/know-your-customers-jobs-to-be-done)
  People "hire" products to make progress on a job. Use for: framing problems instead of features.
