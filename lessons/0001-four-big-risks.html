The Four Big Risks

Before you build an app, work out how it's most likely to fail.

Every app idea is a bet. Marty Cagan says each bet has four ways to go wrong, and good product teams deal with them before building, not after1:

ValueWill anyone actually choose to use it?
UsabilityCan people figure out how to use it?
FeasibilityCan you build it with your time, skills, and tools?
ViabilityCan you afford and sustain it (cost, legal, your time)?
"Tackle the big risks early – especially value risk and business risk." … "The toughest risk is usually value risk."
Marty Cagan, The Four Big Risks

Why this matters for you: coding agents make feasibility much cheaper. You can build almost anything in a weekend. That leaves value as the main way your projects fail: you build something that works well and nobody uses it. This is our reading of Cagan's point for solo builders, not something he says himself.
